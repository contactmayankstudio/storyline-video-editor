Privacy Policy for Storyline

Last updated: 2026-06-15

What we collect
- Anonymous analytics: event names and simple parameters such as feature usage and timestamps.
- Crash reports: stack traces and device information such as OS version, app version, and device model.
- Advertising data: Google AdMob may process advertising identifiers, approximate location, device information, ad interactions, and diagnostics to provide, limit, measure, and improve ads.

What we do NOT collect
- We do NOT collect your name, email, contacts, photos, videos, or other personal data for upload.
- We do NOT access your microphone or camera unless you explicitly use a feature that requires them.

Local processing
- Video editing, rendering and export are performed locally on your device. Your video files are not uploaded by the app.

How data is used
- Analytics help us improve the app and understand feature usage.
- Crash logs help us fix stability issues.
- Ads support the free version of the app and may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your device and Google settings.
- Data is processed by Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics) and Google AdMob under their terms.

Sharing and selling
- We do NOT sell your data.
- Google AdMob receives limited advertising and device data as described above so ads can be served, measured, and protected from fraud.

GDPR and privacy rights
- We collect minimal analytics, crash logs, and ad-related data needed to operate the app and ads.
- You can control ad personalization from Android or Google ad settings where available.
- If you are in the EU, UK, or another region with privacy rights and want help with access or deletion requests, contact the developer through the app store listing.

Contact
Developer contact: use the support contact shown on the app store listing.
