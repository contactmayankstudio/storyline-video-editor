Privacy Policy for Storyline

Last updated: 2026-02-07

What we collect
- Anonymous analytics: event names and simple parameters (feature usage, timestamps).
- Crash reports: stack traces and device info to diagnose crashes (may include OS version and device model).

What we do NOT collect
- We do NOT collect your name, email, contacts, photos, videos, or other personal data for upload.
- We do NOT access your microphone or camera unless you explicitly use a feature that requires them.

Local processing
- Video editing, rendering and export are performed locally on your device. Your video files are not uploaded by the app.

How data is used
- Analytics help us improve the app and understand feature usage.
- Crash logs help us fix stability issues.
- Data is stored and processed by Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics) under their terms.

Sharing and selling
- We do NOT sell your data. We do not share personal data with third parties for advertising.

GDPR and privacy rights
- We only collect minimal anonymous analytics and crash logs. If you are in the EU and want data removed, contact the developer and we will assist.
- Firebase may collect identifiers; review Firebase privacy docs for details.
