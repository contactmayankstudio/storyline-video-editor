Storyline Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-15

What We Collect

  • Anonymous analytics, including event names and simple parameters such as feature usage and timestamps.
  • Crash reports, including stack traces and device information such as OS version, app version, and device model.
  • Advertising data processed by Google AdMob, such as advertising identifiers, approximate location, device information, ad interactions, and diagnostics.

What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, videos, or other personal files for upload.
  • We do not access your microphone or camera unless you explicitly use a feature that requires them.

Local Processing

Video editing, rendering, and export are performed locally on your device. Your video files are not uploaded by Storyline.

How Data Is Used

  • Analytics help us improve the app and understand feature usage.
  • Crash logs help us fix stability issues.
  • Ads support the free version of the app and may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your device and Google settings.
  • Data is processed by Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and Google AdMob under their terms.

Sharing And Selling

We do not sell your data. Google AdMob receives limited advertising and device data so ads can be served, measured, and protected from fraud.

Your Choices

You can control ad personalization from Android or Google ad settings where available. You can also contact the developer through the app store listing for privacy requests.

Contact

Use the support contact shown on the app store listing.
