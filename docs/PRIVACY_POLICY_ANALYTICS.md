## 📋 PRIVACY POLICY TEXT

### Main Privacy Policy Section

**ANALYTICS & USAGE DATA**

We respect your privacy. We collect only minimal, anonymized data to understand how our app is used and to identify and fix problems.

#### What We Collect

We collect the following non-personal, anonymized data:

1. **App Usage**
   - When you open the app (date, time, rough location of session)
   - How long you use the app (session duration)

2. **Video Imports**
   - Video file format (e.g., "mp4", "mov", "webm")
   - Approximate video duration (rounded to nearest second)
   - *We do NOT collect video filenames, paths, or content*

3. **Preview & Playback**
   - When you press the play button
   - *We do NOT track which part of the video you watch or how long*

4. **Export Activity**
   - When you start an export
   - Target export resolution (e.g., "1920x1080")
   - Target frame rate (24fps, 30fps, 60fps)
   - Whether export succeeded or failed
   - Time taken to export
   - Approximate final video duration (rounded to nearest second)
   - General error reason if export fails (e.g., "insufficient storage")

#### What We DO NOT Collect

We explicitly DO NOT collect:

- ❌ Video content, frames, thumbnails, or analysis
- ❌ Audio content, waveforms, or samples
- ❌ Video file paths or file names
- ❌ Your identity, name, email, or phone number
- ❌ Your location or device identifiers (IMEI, MAC address, Android ID)
- ❌ Effects applied, text overlays, or timeline structure
- ❌ Detailed interaction tracking (specific button taps, scroll positions)
- ❌ Device hardware details beyond resolution/FPS preferences
- ❌ Battery level, network type, or cellular data
- ❌ Camera or microphone access logs
- ❌ Any data that could identify you personally

#### Why We Collect This Data

We use this data solely to:

1. **Understand Usage Patterns**
   - How many daily/monthly active users we have
   - Which features are most commonly used
   - Whether users preview before exporting

2. **Identify and Fix Problems**
   - Which devices/Android versions have issues
   - Why exports fail (storage full, codec errors, etc.)
   - Performance problems on specific hardware

3. **Improve Performance**
   - Measure export speed on different hardware
   - Identify which resolution/FPS combinations work best
   - Optimize for the devices users actually have

4. **Prioritize Development**
   - Focus on features that users actually use
   - Fix bugs that impact the most users
   - Optimize for common use cases

#### How Your Data is Protected

- **Anonymous**: You are assigned a random ID. We never connect data to your real identity.
- **Third-Party Security**: Google (Firebase) stores data securely with encryption.
- **No Tracking Across Apps**: Data is not shared with other apps or advertisers.
- **No Retention**: Data is aggregated and older data is automatically deleted after standard retention periods.

#### Your Privacy Rights

**You can opt-out of analytics at any time:**

**On Android:**
1. Open Settings → Privacy → Safety Controls
2. Turn off "Analytics" or "Ad personalization"
3. This disables analytics collection across your device

**In Our App (optional—not yet implemented, but will be in next update):**
1. Settings → Privacy
2. Toggle "Send Usage Analytics" OFF
3. No further data will be collected

**You have the right to:**
- Know what data we collect
- Request deletion of your data
- Opt-out of future collection
- Port your data to other services

#### Third-Party Services

**Firebase Analytics** (by Google)
- Purpose: Collect and aggregate usage data
- Data Handling: Encrypted, stored securely
- Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/terms/analytics
- Terms: https://firebase.google.com/terms

**No Other Third Parties**
- We do not share data with advertisers, analytics brokers, or data brokers
- Firebase (Google) is the only third party that sees this data
- Google's privacy policy applies to data handling

#### Data Retention

- **Raw Events**: Stored for 60 days, then deleted
- **Aggregated Reports**: Kept indefinitely (cannot identify individuals)
- **User Opt-Out**: Respected immediately; no future collection

#### Changes to This Policy

If we change how we collect data:
- We will update this Privacy Policy
- We will highlight changes clearly
- We will give users time to opt-out before changes take effect
- We will NOT start collecting new types of data without notice

#### Contact Us

If you have questions about our privacy practices:
- Email: privacy@videoeditor.example.com
- Or request through the app: Settings → Privacy → Contact Us

## 🔒 GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

### Legal Basis
- **Legal Basis**: Legitimate Interest (Art. 6(1)(f))
  - We have a legitimate interest in understanding how users interact with our app
  - We minimize data collection to protect user privacy
  - We do not use data for profiling or automated decision-making

### Data Subject Rights (GDPR Articles)
Users can exercise the following rights:

1. **Right of Access** (Art. 15)
   - Request what data we have about them
   - We will provide aggregated analytics (cannot identify individuals)

2. **Right to Deletion** (Art. 17)
   - Request deletion of their data
   - We will delete within 30 days

3. **Right to Opt-Out** (Art. 21)
   - Withdraw consent to analytics
   - Changes take effect immediately

4. **Right to Portability** (Art. 20)
   - Request data in machine-readable format
   - We will provide JSON export

5. **Right to Correction** (Art. 16)
   - Request correction of inaccurate data
   - We will update records within 30 days

### Data Processing Agreement (DPA)
- ✅ Firebase has a DPA in place with Google
- ✅ Data is processed with appropriate safeguards
- ✅ Subprocessors disclosed (Google infrastructure)
- ✅ Data transfers comply with Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)

### Breach Notification
- In the unlikely event of a data breach, we will:
  - Notify users within 72 hours
  - Describe what data was affected
  - Explain steps we're taking to remediate
  - Provide contact information for questions

## 🇺🇸 CCPA Compliance (California Users)

### Consumer Rights (CCPA § 1798.100)

1. **Right to Know** (§ 1798.100(a)(1))
   - You have the right to know what personal information we collect
   - We collect only: usage data (no personal information per CCPA)

2. **Right to Delete** (§ 1798.100(a)(2))
   - You have the right to deletion of personal information
   - Submit request through Settings → Privacy → Request Deletion
   - We will delete within 45 days

3. **Right to Opt-Out of Sale** (§ 1798.100(a)(3))
   - **We DO NOT sell your data**
   - We do not share data with data brokers or advertisers
   - No opt-out needed

4. **Right to Non-Discrimination** (§ 1798.100(a)(4))
   - We do not discriminate based on exercise of CCPA rights
   - Using privacy controls will not affect app functionality

### Categories of Data Collected (CCPA § 1798.140)

**Not Collected (CCPA Personal Information)**:
- Identifiers (name, email, phone, address)
- Commercial information (purchase history, credit card)
- Biometric information
- Internet activity (browsing history, search history)
- Geolocation information
- Sensory information (audio/video recordings)
- Professional information
- Education information
- Inferences (profiling, credit score)

**Collected (Non-Personal)**:
- Usage patterns (app sessions, features used)
- Technical data (video format, export resolution)
- Approximate performance data (export time)

### CalOPPA Compliance (Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code § 22575)

- ✅ **Website**: [VideoEditor.example.com](https://example.com)
- ✅ **Email**: privacy@videoeditor.example.com
- ✅ **Do Not Track**: Honored (user can disable analytics in Settings)

### Sale of Personal Information

**We DO NOT sell personal information.**

We do not:
- Collect and sell personal information to data brokers
- Sell information to advertisers or marketing firms
- Share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising
- Trade information for anything of value
